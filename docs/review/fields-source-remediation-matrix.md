## Statements removed or narrowed in the revised pack

- No field claims that a course is available, approved, recognised, eligible, affordable, current, or linked to a guaranteed outcome.
- Healthcare no longer uses medical regulation as evidence for all health professions.
- Arts and design no longer treats architecture regulation as evidence for all design education.
- Technology and engineering do not imply that every related course follows AICTE approval rules.
- Law, education and regulated healthcare sections tell students to verify the exact current programme and authority.
- Government, hospitality-tourism and social-sciences retain no unsupported named pathway or regulator claims.

## Publication decision

Every field remains `draft` / `pending_review`. A reviewer may eventually approve authored guidance after checking tone and safety, but no record should be marked `verified` merely because it has a source link. For source-backed context, verify the exact claim, link availability, source freshness and appropriate claim key at review time.
