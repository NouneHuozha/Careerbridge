# Pathways Development Pilot — Source Evidence

Checked on 2026-11-25. These first-party pages are suitable for claim-level provenance in the initial development pilot. The Pathways records remain drafts until their evidence and lifecycle are reviewed in the development database; this note is not publication approval.

## National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS) Senior Secondary

Official NIOS course/admissions page: https://www.nios.ac.in/online-course-material/secondary-courses/admission-detail-for-various-courses.aspx

The page states that NIOS Senior Secondary is designed for learners who have passed Class X or equivalent from a recognised board and want to continue toward a Senior Secondary certification equivalent to Class XII. It says learners choose from the course subject scheme and must successfully complete at least five subjects for certification. This source supports the basic open-schooling route, its Class X entry relationship, subject selection, and qualification description. The page contains additional admission details; avoid importing fees, current seat availability, or time-sensitive application details into this pathway.

Official NIOS FAQ: https://sdmis.nios.ac.in/home/faqs

This FAQ describes online admission options and confirms that a recognized Secondary certificate is needed for NIOS Senior Secondary admission. It is corroborating evidence for the route’s admission caveat.

## Directorate General of Training (DGT), Craftsmen Training Scheme

Official scheme page: https://dgt.gov.in/en/CTS

DGT describes CTS as a vocational training scheme delivered through ITIs, distinguishes central responsibility for national policy, standards, exams and certification from State/UT responsibility for day-to-day ITI administration and admissions, and describes current trades/courses as 1- or 2-year durations. The page says CTS includes 169 NSQF-compliant trades, but that figure is time-sensitive and should not be asserted in a student pathway without a dated check.

Official trade list: https://dgt.gov.in/en/cts-details

The list gives trade-by-trade minimum qualifications and durations. Examples show that entry criteria differ by trade: some listed courses require Class 8; others Class 10, sometimes with Science/Mathematics; and durations vary. It supports a broad post-Class-10 exploration statement only when the pathway explicitly says to verify each trade’s current entry conditions.

## DGT Apprenticeship Training

Official overview: https://dgt.gov.in/en/Apprenticeship_Training

DGT states that workplace learning supplements institutional training, describes five categories of apprentices, and says trade-apprentice qualifications range from Class VIII through Class XII. It describes trade apprenticeship training as basic training and practical training with related instruction, and requires an apprenticeship contract. This supports a broad, category-specific work-based training overview, but not a guarantee of an apprenticeship place, employment, stipend amount, or eligibility for a particular opportunity.
